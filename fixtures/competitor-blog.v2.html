Introducing Realtime Voice Agents

August 12, 2026 Priya Raman

Today we are shipping realtime voice agents with sub-300ms latency. Building a production voice agent used to mean stitching together a speech recogniser, a language model and a text-to-speech engine, then fighting the seams between them for weeks.

Our new pipeline collapses that into a single streaming call. You describe the agent in plain language, point it at your tools, and it answers the phone. Latency is the headline, but the part teams tell us matters more is interruption handling.
